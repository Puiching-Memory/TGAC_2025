# SQL生成任务
## 用户问题
统计20250702手游fps最后活跃日期为近半年的用户的城市等级分布。
输出：城市等级，用户人数
## 涉及表名
`dim_mgamejp_account_allinfo_nf`, `dim_uf_player_gameinfo_mf`
## 提示信息
用户固定筛选逻辑:
and saccounttype = "-100" -- 账户类型为-100
and suseridtype in ("qq","wxid") -- 用户类型为qq或wxid
and sgamecode in ("esports","mobile_live","initiatived","allianceforce","strategy","jordass","su","playzone","battlezone","battlearena","weapon","playhub","manager","apgame") -- 游戏代码为指定的手游fps
and splattype = "-100" -- 平台类型为-100
活跃日期逻辑:
基于当前统计时间20250702向前计算6个月的登陆日期，即ilastactdate >= "20250104"
城市等级分布：
当前时间为20250702，城市等级分布的相关表是月表，所以需要取kp_imp_date = "202506"的数据作为关联数据
## 成功案例参考
以下案例执行成功，可作为参考：
### 成功案例 1
- **SQL ID**: `sql_9`
- **得分**: 1
- **来源**: CKPT `V7.1_22.09_1108`

**SQL:**

```sql
SELECT t2.city_level, COUNT(*) AS user_count FROM dim_mgamejp_account_allinfo_nf t1 JOIN dim_uf_player_gameinfo_mf t2 ON t1.suserid = t2.userid WHERE t1.dtstatdate = 20250702 AND t1.saccounttype = '-100' AND t1.suseridtype IN ('qq', 'wxid') AND t1.sgamecode IN ('esports', 'mobile_live', 'initiatived', 'allianceforce', 'strategy', 'jordass', 'su', 'playzone', 'battlezone', 'battlearena', 'weapon', 'playhub', 'manager', 'apgame') AND t1.splattype = '-100' AND t1.ilastactdate >= 20250104 AND t2.kp_imp_date = '202506' GROUP BY t2.city_level;
```

### 成功案例 2
- **SQL ID**: `sql_9`
- **得分**: 1
- **来源**: CKPT `V6_31.40_1105`

**SQL:**

```sql
select 
    city_level,
    count(*) as user_count
from dim_mgamejp_account_allinfo_nf a
join dim_uf_player_gameinfo_mf b
    on a.suserid = b.userid
where a.saccounttype = '-100'
    and a.suseridtype in ('qq','wxid')
    and a.sgamecode in ('esports','mobile_live','initiatived','allianceforce','strategy','jordass','su','playzone','battlezone','battlearena','weapon','playhub','manager','apgame')
    and a.splattype = '-100'
    and a.ilastactdate >= 20250104
    and a.dtstatdate = 20250702
    and b.kp_imp_date = '202506'
    and b.id_type in ('qq','wx')
    and b.userid in (select suserid from dim_mgamejp_account_allinfo_nf where dtstatdate = 20250702 and saccounttype = '-100' and suseridtype in ('qq','wxid') and sgamecode in ('esports','mobile_live','initiatived','allianceforce','strategy','jordass','su','playzone','battlezone','battlearena','weapon','playhub','manager','apgame') and splattype = '-100' and ilastactdate >= 20250104)
 group by city_level
```
