# SQL生成任务
## 用户问题
统计2025年1月勇者盟约端游活跃玩家交叉峡谷端游及手游活跃玩家
输出：玩家gplayerid
## 涉及表名
`dws_argothek_oss_login_di`, `dim_argothek_gplayerid2qqwxid_df`, `dws_mgamejp_login_user_activity_di`
## 提示信息
峡谷筛选逻辑:
sgamecode = "initiatived" -- 筛选峡谷游戏
and saccounttype = "-100" -- 账号体系，取-100表示汇总
and suseridtype in ("qq", "wxid") -- 账号类型，取qq或wxid
and splattype in ("-100", "PC") -- 峡谷手游玩家及PC端玩家
and splat = "-100" -- 写死为-100
## 成功案例参考
以下案例执行成功，可作为参考：
### 成功案例 1
- **SQL ID**: `sql_3`
- **得分**: 1
- **来源**: CKPT `V6_31.40_1105`

**SQL:**

```sql
SELECT DISTINCT b.iuserid as gplayerid
FROM dws_argothek_oss_login_di a
JOIN dim_argothek_gplayerid2qqwxid_df b ON a.iuserid = b.iuserid
JOIN dws_mgamejp_login_user_activity_di c ON b.suserid = c.suserid
WHERE a.statis_date >= 20250101 AND a.statis_date <= 20250131
  AND c.sgamecode = 'initiatived'
  AND c.saccounttype = '-100'
  AND c.suseridtype IN ('qq', 'wxid')
  AND c.splattype IN ('-100', 'PC')
  AND c.splat = '-100'
  AND c.dtstatdate >= 20250101 AND c.dtstatdate <= 20250131
```
