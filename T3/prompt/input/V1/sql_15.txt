统计2025年5月勇者盟约流失用户中，流向峡谷PC的玩家gplayerid

## 涉及表名
`dws_argothek_oss_useractivity_df`, `dim_argothek_gplayerid2qqwxid_df`, `dws_mgamejp_login_user_activity_di`

and saccounttype = "-100" -- 账号体系，取-100表示汇总

and splattype = "PC" -- 峡谷端游玩家
and splat = "-100" -- 写死为-100
5月流失：限定INSTR(SUBSTR(REVERSE(RPAD(iactivity,128,'0')),1,31),'1') = 0 AND INSTR(SUBSTR(REVERSE(RPAD(iactivity,128,'0')),32,31),'1') > 0
取20250608的数据做账号转换
