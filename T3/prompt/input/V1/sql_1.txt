# SQL生成任务
## 用户问题
统计2025.07.24的手游全量用户且标签为其他，在竞品业务下2025.05.30-2025.07.24的在线时长。
输出：suserid、sgamecode、ionlinetime


## 涉及表名
`dws_mgamejp_login_user_activity_di`, `dim_vplayerid_vies_df`
## 提示信息
竞品业务：
sgamecode in ("initiatived","jordass","esports","allianceforce","strategy","playzone","su")
saccounttype = "-100" -- 账号体系，取-100表示汇总
and suseridtype in ("qq","wxid") -- 用户类型
and splattype = "-100" -- 平台类型
and splat = "-100" -- 平台，写死为-100
## 成功案例参考
以下案例执行成功，可作为参考：
### 成功案例 1
- **SQL ID**: `sql_1`
- **得分**: 1
- **来源**: CKPT `V7.1_22.09_1108`

**SQL:**

```sql
SELECT /*+ SET_VAR(query_timeout=300) */ a.suserid, a.sgamecode, SUM(a.ionlinetime) as total_ionlinetime
FROM dws_mgamejp_login_user_activity_di a
JOIN dim_vplayerid_vies_df b ON a.suserid = b.suserid AND b.dtstatdate = '20250724'
WHERE a.dtstatdate BETWEEN 20250530 AND 20250724
  AND b.itag = '其他'
  AND a.sgamecode IN ('initiatived','jordass','esports','allianceforce','strategy','playzone','su')
  AND a.saccounttype = '-100'
  AND a.suseridtype IN ('qq','wxid')
  AND a.splattype = '-100'
  AND a.splat = '-100'
GROUP BY a.suserid, a.sgamecode
ORDER BY a.suserid, a.sgamecode;
```
