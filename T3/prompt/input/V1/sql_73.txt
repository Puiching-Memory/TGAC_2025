# SQL生成任务
## 用户问题
统计20241123-20241222对决冠军，乐园其他，和乐园汇总 三类用户的组队和对局情况
输出：乐园总参与人数，乐园总参与局数，乐园组队人数，乐园组队局数，对决冠军总参与人数，对决冠军总参与局数，对决冠军组队人数，对决冠军组队局数，乐园其他总参与人数，乐园其他总参与局数，乐园其他组队人数，乐园其他组队局数
## 涉及表名
`dwd_jordass_player_matchrecord_hi`
## 提示信息
对决冠军：matchmode=1003 and matchsubmodegroup=1345785623905087744
乐园其他：matchmode=1003 and matchsubmodegroup<>1345785623905087744
乐园汇总：matchmode=1003
都需要限制 matchresult="ok"
组队：teamnum>1
## 成功案例参考

### 成功案例 1
- **SQL ID**: `sql_73`
- **得分**: 1
- **来源**: CKPT `V7.1_22.09_1108`

**SQL:**

```sql
SELECT
    -- 乐园汇总 (matchmode=1003, matchresult='ok')
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园总参与人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 乐园总参与局数,
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园组队人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 乐园组队局数,

    -- 对决冠军 (matchmode=1003, matchsubmodegroup=1345785623905087744, matchresult='ok')
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN vplayerid END) AS 对决冠军总参与人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 对决冠军总参与局数,
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN vplayerid END) AS 对决冠军组队人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 对决冠军组队局数,

    -- 乐园其他 (matchmode=1003, matchsubmodegroup<>1345785623905087744, matchresult='ok')
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup <> 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园其他总参与人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup <> 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 乐园其他总参与局数,
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup <> 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园其他组队人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup <> 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND teamnum > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 乐园其他组队局数
FROM dwd_jordass_player_matchrecord_hi
WHERE tdbank_imp_date BETWEEN '20241123' AND '20241222'
```

### 成功案例 2

```sql
SELECT
    -- 乐园总参与人数，乐园总参与局数，乐园组队人数，乐园组队局数

    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 乐园总参与局数,

    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND CAST(teamnum AS BIGINT) > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 乐园组队局数,
    
    -- 对决冠军总参与人数，对决冠军总参与局数，对决冠军组队人数，对决冠军组队局数
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN vplayerid END) AS 对决冠军总参与人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 对决冠军总参与局数,
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND CAST(teamnum AS BIGINT) > 1 THEN vplayerid END) AS 对决冠军组队人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup = 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND CAST(teamnum AS BIGINT) > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 对决冠军组队局数,
    
    -- 乐园其他总参与人数，乐园其他总参与局数，乐园其他组队人数，乐园其他组队局数
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup != 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园其他总参与人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup != 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' THEN 1 END) AS 乐园其他总参与局数,
    COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup != 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND CAST(teamnum AS BIGINT) > 1 THEN vplayerid END) AS 乐园其他组队人数,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN matchmode = 1003 AND matchsubmodegroup != 1345785623905087744 AND matchresult = 'ok' AND CAST(teamnum AS BIGINT) > 1 THEN 1 END) AS 乐园其他组队局数
FROM dwd_jordass_player_matchrecord_hi
WHERE tdbank_imp_date >= '2024112300'
  AND tdbank_imp_date <= '2024122223'
  AND matchmode = 1003
  AND matchresult = 'ok'
```
